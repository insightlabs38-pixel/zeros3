# Z4-P0 bottleneck register

Evidence tags: **[M]** measured by P0 on the real engine; **[P]** profile (CPU/alloc/block/mutex) in `results/prof/`; **[S]** strace syscall counts; **[X]** micro-experiment; **[E]** estimate (Amdahl on measured shares, not a promise).
Environment: 4 vCPU Firecracker VM, ext4 on virtio (fsync p50 0.26 ms: a *cheap* fsync, so storage-bound findings are a lower bound for real devices). Gains are end-to-end ranges, never measured speedups.
`results/` paths are relative to this directory. Confidence: H = mechanism and share both measured; M = mechanism measured, gain extrapolated; L = hypothesis.
