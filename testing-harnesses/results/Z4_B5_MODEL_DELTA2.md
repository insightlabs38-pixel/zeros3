**Short answer.** Exact compression of genuinely evolving weights is dominated by *first-sight float coding*, not by history: a new exponent-rANS coder plus exact F32→F16 narrowing already stores a Pythia-70M checkpoint at 0.70× production CDC; adding the best of up to 3 pooled prior checkpoints (multi-base, content-discovered, hop-bounded ≤3) reaches 0.655×, i.e. only 6–7 % from evolution. Fine-tunes help more (SmolLM2 SFT/DPO star 0.567×, Qwen2.5-0.5B base→Instruct→Coder/abliterated 0.568×, GPT-2 SFT→PPO 0.727×) but only 4–19 % better than the strongest existing alternative (B1 aligned-chunk PXR oracle / lab Model kind 8); on Qwen the B1 PXR oracle is *ahead* of B5's single-base policies (0.698 vs 0.716) and B5 passes it only with pooled bases plus the low-rank hybrid (0.568). The large wins are **structured**: rank‑1 edits (abliteration) cost 1.2 % of direct coding, scalings 0.3 %, LoRA-like updates 2.1× smaller with the low-rank ladder, and **quantized GGUF families whose floats exist cost 0.28–0.41× CDC** because Q8_0/Q5_0/Q5_1/Q4_K/Q6_K block quantizers reproduce byte-exactly. Natural SGD/AdamW noise is incompressible beyond exponent-conditioned residual coding: affine, low-rank and multi-base predictors give ≤2 % on Pythia/SmolLM2/Qwen natural histories. All savings are *authoritative-only*: additive sidecar is 1.57–1.79× CDC.

Findings. (1) Weight updates are noise at the ULP scale: after exponent conditioning the typical Pythia residual is 10 bits/16 and shared scaling cannot remove it (a least‑squares affine fit is *worse* than identity — it chases the few large movers — until coefficients are chosen by a residual bit‑length proxy; robust affine then gains 0.7 %). (2) Low‑rank prediction fails on every natural history (SVD of the delta captures noise) but **recovers exact structure**: abliteration's rank‑1 projection (46 tensors) and the LoRA control (r=8, factors charged at 4 B/elem) — and the ladder matters: r=16 overfits the rounding noise (0.339 vs r=8 0.303 of raw) so the rank/size frontier is search‑selected per tensor. (3) Affine is exact for pure scalings (0.97× control: 607 KB of 270 MB). (4) Multi‑base: causal extrapolation/anchor gives 1.6–2.0 % on Pythia; non‑causal interpolation gives 7.2 % — an upper bound for B‑frame style retrospective optimisation, not an online policy. In SmolLM2 the DPO stage is identified by pooling (Instruct from SFT‑only+base: 7.3 MB). (5) Headroom: NRC is already below the plane‑conditioned entropy bound (10–18 % below DEFLATE planes, §7) and the non‑causal interpolation oracle is the only stronger predictor found, so within this predictor family natural histories have no further headroom; further gains need training‑side information (optimizer state, data order). Predictions are float64 with explicit conversions (no FMA), non‑finite coefficients fall back to the base value, residuals are exact bijections on all bit patterns (NaN payloads, ±0, subnormals tested).
