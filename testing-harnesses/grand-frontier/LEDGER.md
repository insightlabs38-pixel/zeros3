# Grand Frontier ledger

| Pillar | State | Evidence and remaining work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GF0 | Baseline frozen | Nine families, paired native stores, exact reopen and reads. |
| GF1 | Partial, measured | Exact and bounded search with native signed materialization, hard work caps and a held-out counterexample. Calibrated online selection and arbitrary layout optimization remain open. |
| GF2 | Specialized opt-in | Signed space and whole-read advantage on the first gzip pair. Native held-out decline, mixed tails, repair, crash and downgrade are recorded. |
| GF3 | Research only | Shared pages save space on the controlled cohort and reclaim exactly. Fixed programs lose on real histories across budgets. |
| GF4 | Research only | Exact offline comparator and signed concurrent hot-copy trials. The controller missed the tail gate and did not consistently beat LRU. |
| GF5 | Research only | True checkpoint lineage, reversible artifacts and physical pruning. Native ZN7 remains the space and whole-read incumbent. |
| GF6 | Specialized research | Signed one- and two-base selection, exact retry and shaped crossover. Coordinator stays opt-in in the test lab. |
| GF7 | Specialized research | Two executable token translators, signed held-out native packs, feature gating and crash validation. |
| GF8 | Research qualified | Independently rebuilt held-out and mixed stores, scoped repair, process fences and bounded race and fuzz. Deployment gates are explicit. |

Both experimental readers are qualified for the measured scope. See the
