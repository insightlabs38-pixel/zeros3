# GF3 multi-source phrase factoring

Compare single-source, bounded multi-source, and mined immutable phrase
banks under the same independently verified object ranges. FB-14's native
XD search and physical chunk baselines are the incumbent for TypeScript
and Parquet. Preserve its observed placements where possible.

Start with a controlled exact union oracle and a correlated release/edit
panel. Determine whether additional candidate recall earns physical savings.
Measure native regional fingerprints, shallow vs hierarchical sketches,
and a bounded anchor index against exhaustive small candidate search.
Include low-similarity targets built from multiple unrelated sources so
the relevant matches can survive a small whole-object similarity score.

For phrase banks, choose repeated substrings whose removal would reduce
the exclusive compressed representation cost across several independently
live objects. Price the phrase exactly once, including indexes and native
pack costs, with optional local copies also counted. Compare different
boundary and pack assignments. A volume page, exact object, or arbitrary
unaligned phrase can be an anchor. Chronological direction and publication
depth remain independently bounded.

Freeze discovery caps and placement choices before applying to HTTPX or a
separate lineage. Verify release when no program still names a phrase.
Adversarial controls cover entropy, high churn, disappeared parents,
cross-block ranges and partial publication.
